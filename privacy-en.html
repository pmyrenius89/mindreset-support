Privacy Policy – Mindreset

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Mindreset is built to collect as little about you as possible. This page explains exactly what's handled, and by whom.

We do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact details
  • Your location
  • Health data or the reason you're using the app
  • Contacts, photos, or other data from your phone

There is no account to create in Mindreset.

What's stored locally on your device

  • Your volume preference
  • Whether you've unlocked Focus, Relax, and Sleep

This information never leaves your phone to reach us.

Purchases and payment

If you choose to unlock Focus, Relax, and Sleep, the purchase itself is handled by Apple (the App Store) and RevenueCat, our purchase-verification provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated identifier tied to your purchase — never your name or email address. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy here: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Free codes

If you redeem a free code, the code and an anonymous identifier are sent to our server, which checks that the code is valid and then unlocks the app. The code and identifier aren't used for anything else.

Advertising and tracking

Mindreset shows no ads and doesn't share data with ad networks or marketing services.

Children

Mindreset is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
