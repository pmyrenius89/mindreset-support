Mindreset – Support

Frequently asked questions and how to reach us.

How do I unlock Focus, Relax, and Sleep?

Reset is always free. The other three modes unlock with a one-time purchase (no subscription) directly in the app — tap the "Unlock" button at the top right of the player screen.

I switched phones / reinstalled the app — how do I get my purchase back?

Open the paywall (tap a locked mode, or the "Unlock" chip) and choose "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

The sound stops when I lock my screen or switch apps

Mindreset is built to keep playing in the background. If it still stops: check that the app has permission to run in the background under Settings → Mindreset on your phone, and that another app isn't blocking the "Audio" playback mode.

I have a free code — how do I redeem it?

Open the paywall and tap "Have a code?" at the bottom, enter the code, and tap "Redeem".

Mindreset is not a medical product

Mindreset is a sound and relaxation app, not a medical treatment, and it doesn't replace professional care. If you're managing ADHD, stress, or sleep issues, please talk to a healthcare provider.
